This is your foundation for offering services to bail bondsmen. Your goal: deliver actionable leads (recent social activity, new accounts, associate networks, vehicle info, photo metadata) using only public sources so the bondsman can act quickly and legally.

What Bondsmen Actually Want (Pitch This)

  • Recent digital footprint (posts from last 7-30 days = current area)
  • New or reactivated social/email accounts (sign of new location or alias)
  • Associates / family / romantic interests map
  • Vehicle sightings or plates from public posts
  • Photos/videos with metadata or background clues
  • Clean, timestamped report they can use or hand to law enforcement

Fedora 44 KDE Specific Setup

  • Always connected to VPN
  • Use Zen Browser or a fresh Firefox profile (no personal logins, uBlock Origin enabled)
  • Launch tools from your OSINT folder on the bottom panel
  • Store cases in ~/OSINT/Cases/fugitive-LASTNAME-DATE/
  • Use Konsole with --hold for long-running commands

Legal & Business Reminder: Only use publicly available data. Get everything in writing with the bondsman. Document chain of custody. Never confront the jumper yourself unless you are licensed to do so in your jurisdiction.
